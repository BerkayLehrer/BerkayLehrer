We Are With You on Your Journey to Germany

As BK Partners, we provide legal consultancy services in the fields of family law, divorce, inheritance, immigration, and foreign nationals law. In addition, we provide Germany immigration consultancy services to individuals who want to settle in Germany.

Immigration to Germany: Not Just an Application, but a Roadmap

Immigrating to Germany is often a complex and daunting process. Which path is right, where to start, and the consequences of possible errors can be challenging without professional support.

We plan the Germany visa and immigration process step by step together, especially in the following areas:

  • Immigration to Germany through Ausbildung (vocational training)
  • Germany work visa and qualified workforce immigration
  • EU Blue Card applications
  • Germany student visa applications
  • Germany family reunification visa
  • Germany foreign nationals law applications and appeals

The immigration process to Germany requires the joint evaluation of the right profession choice, equivalence procedures, language level, and visa stages. Our goal is to make this process more understandable, safer, and less stressful for you.

Germany-Related Family, Divorce, and Inheritance Law

Family law cases coincide with the most sensitive periods in people's lives. Therefore, our approach in family law, divorce, and inheritance cases is always clear:

Open communication, realistic expectations, and a solid legal foundation.

Especially in Germany-related marriage, divorce, and inheritance law matters, working with a lawyer who knows the laws of both countries ensures a healthier progression of the process.

What to Expect When Working With Us?

  • Solutions specific to your situation, not based on ready-made templates
  • Understandable language and clear legal answers
  • Transparent information at every stage of the process
  • Legal support from lawyers who know German practice

For us, each case is not just a legal procedure, but an important turning point in a person's life. Our goal is not to leave you alone on your journey to Germany and to carry out the process together with confidence.
